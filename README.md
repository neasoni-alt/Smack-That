## 🧸 A little story

The teddy starts with a broken heart. Each tap brings a different bat swing and a new reaction: a scratch, a bumped head, then a dramatic crying fall.

But the story has a softer ending. The teddy gets back up, smiles, and holds a healed heart. Give it a hug, and the whole scene starts fresh.

> Still cute after everything. Still ready for a hug. ♡

## 🏏 How to play

Tap or click the **bat**, the **teddy**, or the **main button**.

| Moment | Teddy’s reaction |
| --- | --- |
| Hit 1 | A bruise, scratches, and an “Ouch!” |
| Hit 2 | A bump, a bandage, and a paw holding its head |
| Hit 3 | A crying fall with extra tears |
| Recovery | Teddy gets back up smiling with a healed heart |
| Healing hug | The hit counter resets and you can play again |

The three reactions happen in the main scene. There are no separate reaction cards. Extra taps during a swing are ignored so the animation stays in order.

## ✨ Small details, big feelings

- Three different bat swing animations.
- Five teddy expressions made from inline SVG.
- Floating hearts, sparkles, impact bursts, and speech bubbles.
- A live hit counter and a healing hug reset.
- Responsive layouts and touch controls for phones.
- Keyboard-friendly buttons and visible focus outlines.
- Reduced animation when the visitor prefers reduced motion.

## 🚀 Run locally

1. Keep `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js` in the same folder.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.
3. Tap the bat and let the tiny drama begin.

No installation or build step is needed. The page uses Google Fonts when available and falls back to local fonts.

**Live preview:** [missing-you-teddy.neasonivodlab.chatgpt.site](https://missing-you-teddy.neasonivodlab.chatgpt.site)

The hosted preview is private and requires access through the owner’s account. The downloaded page can run locally.

## 🎨 Make it yours

| Want to change… | Edit… |
| --- | --- |
| The name, headline, or subtitle | `index.html` |
| Colors, spacing, and bat swings | `style.css` |
| Speech bubbles and hit captions | The `scenes` and `effects` arrays in `script.js` |
| Recovery timing | The third-hit timeout in `script.js` |
| Teddy expressions | The SVG expression groups in `index.html` |

The hit counter is temporary. It resets after a healing hug or a page refresh; the page does not save gameplay data.
